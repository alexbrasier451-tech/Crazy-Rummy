This is deliberately a narrow authority. It must not be read as approval for

## Proposed Stage 0 contract

- **Scope:** v1.1 is a complete, premium presentation overhaul of Crazy Rummy,
  preserving rules, route behaviour, authoritative online truth, privacy
  boundaries, and dependable product operation.
- **Direction:** **The Midnight Limited** — an authored contemporary night-
  train card room: black enamel, deep baize, brass, cream ticket material,
  controlled light, and physical card interaction; not steampunk, casino
  neon, a literal train simulator, or a generic dashboard.
- **Creative standard:** visual ambition is a required deliverable. Generic
  recolouring, decorative MVP preservation, conventional browser cards with
  ornament, or technically polished but forgettable surfaces fail the
  creative-ambition gate.
- **Permitted exploration:** weak MVP presentation structures may be replaced
  in concepts; cards are the authored hero system; major routes and required
  game moments need specific direction; selected concepts arise from at least
  three materially different approaches.
- **Non-negotiable precedence:** game-state truth and privacy; legibility and
  action clarity; accessible, reliable input; and supported-phone performance
  outrank spectacle. Within those limits, cohesion, authored detail, and
  spectacle must be maximised.
- **Free-only production boundary:** no paid plan, payment card, paid asset,
  CDN, third-party runtime asset host, or metered dependency may become
  release-critical. Runtime stays dependency-light and local-first.
- **Asset boundary:** no future production asset is accepted without local
  source/derivative traceability, rights/licence evidence, provenance and
  review records. Generated material is not exempt and must have a documented
  fallback.
- **Owner authority:** no automated, technical, or process evidence overrides
  the owner’s final judgement that the work has pushed the boat out
  sufficiently.

## Stage 1 exit gates before production UI authority

1. Reconcile the active beta chain against the v1.1 route, screen, state,
   privacy, accessibility, and gameplay-flow assumptions; record every
   material delta. This may prepare the record while beta work continues but
   cannot close the production gate before the project owner signs the beta
   complete.
2. Present three materially different structural concepts for both lobby and
   busy six-player live game, with a clear selection/rejection rationale.
3. Present selected 390 × 844 lobby and busy live-game keyframes, the selected
   live-game 320 × 568 and 768 × 900 variants, and reduced-motion and forced-
   colour representations.
4. Present the selected type/palette, card face/back, action hierarchy,
   material-density, route/moment signature map, and proposed asset provenance
   plan.
5. Receive explicit owner approval of the reconciled selected package and a
   separate decision to open production UI implementation. That decision also
   requires the project owner's beta-complete sign-off and reconciliation
   against its exact immutable revision.

Reduced-motion and forced-colour concept representations demonstrate the
intended alternative design treatment only. They do not constitute
implementation, interaction, accessibility, or device evidence.

Stage 1 must record any ambitious treatment it rejects or defers, including the
preferred treatment, concrete blocking constraint, higher-priority requirement
or measured budget at risk, and the strongest compliant alternative. “Too much
work,” “too difficult,” or a simpler existing component are not adequate
reasons.
